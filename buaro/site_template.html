How deadly is heat in Attica, and is it changing?

BUARO turns the Bayesian Urban Adaptation and Resilience Optimization Model of AI for Planetary Resilience (D. Sargiotis, Springer, 2027) into open software that learns from official European data. This page is rebuilt automatically every time the pipeline runs.
